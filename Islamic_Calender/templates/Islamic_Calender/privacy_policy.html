Privacy Policy

MuslimAI is an Islamic companion app for Quran reading and listening, prayer times, qibla direction, adhkar, and related worship tools. This page explains what data the app uses and why.

Effective Date

April 30, 2026

Data We Use

Location data

MuslimAI may use your device location when you allow permission so the app can calculate local prayer times and qibla direction accurately.

Local Preferences

On-device settings

The app stores settings such as last-read Quran position, selected reciter, bookmarks, saved coordinates, and similar usage preferences on your device.

Technical Data

Diagnostics

Standard crash, diagnostic, or platform telemetry may be collected by Apple, device operating systems, or infrastructure providers used to deliver app functionality.

Content Delivery

Third-party services

Quran, prayer, and related Islamic content may be served through third-party APIs or hosting providers that are required to deliver the app experience.

How Data Is Used

Purpose of processing

  • To calculate accurate prayer times and qibla direction.
  • To maintain Quran reading progress, audio selections, and bookmarks.
  • To improve app stability, reliability, and user experience.
  • To deliver Islamic content required by the features you use.

Data Sharing

No sale of personal data

MuslimAI does not sell your personal information. Limited technical or content-delivery data may pass through the service providers required to operate the app.

Your Choices

Control and removal

You can deny or disable location access in your device settings. Removing the app from your device removes locally stored app data from that device.

Contact

Privacy questions

For privacy questions or requests, contact mkhairy@londontrade.ca.
